Chart Notes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Chart Notes ("we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you interact with our products on Etsy and related social-media channels including Pinterest.

Information we collect

  • Order information you provide directly to Etsy when purchasing — name, shipping address, payment. Payment is handled entirely by Etsy and never reaches us.
  • Aggregate analytics from Etsy and from social platforms (Pinterest, Instagram, etc.) showing how shoppers find our listings. This data is anonymized and does not personally identify you.

How we use information

  • To fulfill your order via our print-on-demand partner (Printful).
  • To improve our products and listings.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties beyond what is required to fulfill your order (Etsy and Printful).

Pinterest integration

We use the Pinterest API to publish product pins linking to our Etsy listings. The Pinterest integration does not collect or transmit any data from individual users. Pin engagement analytics are reviewed only in aggregate.

Cookies

Etsy and Pinterest use their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies. We do not set additional cookies of our own.

Your rights

You may request deletion of any personal information we hold about you by emailing the shop owner via Etsy's messaging system.

Contact

Reach us through our Etsy shop's contact form.
